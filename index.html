01Profile

I BUILD.
THEN I TAKE
IT APART.

That's how I learn what a system really does — and how to make it smaller, clearer, and more useful.

I don't collect technologies. I use them to solve the problem in front of me.

I'm Jimsedric, a software developer working across web apps, automation, artificial intelligence, and the infrastructure underneath. My public trail starts in 2015 and runs from PHP systems and HTML experiments to Deno services, canvas tools, and interfaces for local language models.

Most of what I build starts with a small, stubborn question: can this be simpler? That instinct took me from CodeIgniter systems in 2016 to an Ollama chat client and a Firebase payroll dashboard in 2025 — and into automation and self-hosted infrastructure now.
